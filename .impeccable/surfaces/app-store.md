# Storefront (all shopper routes)

Mode: Persuade on home, category and product; Operate on cart, checkout, order and tracking.
Audience: phone shoppers arriving from Instagram/WhatsApp links; job is see it, trust it, order (COD/UPI).
Bar: Nykaa / Nykaa Fashion, Myntra, Amazon / Flipkart. Chosen path: the category standard (canon), played straight.
Constraints: owner theme (colours, font, radius, grid/list) drives every surface; no invented ratings, reviews or sales counts.

## Direction contract
Seed key: 6c439bc2 (canon)

THESIS: The Indian marketplace standard at full craft: banner rail, category tiles, offer chips, dense price-led cards, a product page built around price, MRP and delivery facts. Refuses the empty template look (grey hero box, letter tiles, lone grid).

OWN-WORLD: White ground, owner primary as the one accent at page scale (announcement, CTAs, active chips, sale % off), owner font, owner radius. Hairline dividers, no card borders on products, soft tinted shadow only on sticky bars and sheets. Price row: bold price, struck MRP, "% off" in accent.

STORY: First phone screen shows the store, an offer, categories and products. Every card says price, saving and how to add. Product page answers price, delivery, COD, returns before scroll. Cart shows savings; checkout reads as one calm form with a sticky total.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Announcement bar, compact header with logo, search, bag; banner carousel or a branded hero with real store copy; category tiles; offer chips.

SIGNATURE: Sticky mobile buy bar on the product page and sticky order summary on cart/checkout; add-to-cart gives an instant bag count bump.

RISK: Familiarity is the point; failure is looking like an unfilled template with no photos. No-photo products must still look designed.
